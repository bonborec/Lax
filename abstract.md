For a finite undirected simple graph, the feedback vertex number is the minimum
number of vertices whose deletion makes the graph acyclic, and the feedback
edge number is the corresponding minimum number of edges. We prove that the
feedback vertex number is at most the feedback edge number, and that treewidth
is at most the feedback vertex number plus one. The latter proof adds a minimum
feedback vertex set to every bag of a tree decomposition of the remaining forest.
